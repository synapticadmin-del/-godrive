> **Goal.** Give the operator somewhere to act.

## Why this blocks launch

An SOS alert has no admin surface of any kind (T11 F-11-02) — a rider presses the panic button and the only trace is a phone notification that, once dismissed, is unrecoverable without D1 access. Payout requests are equally unactionable (F-11-04). Both are the operations half of work whose API half is E13 and E06; shipping either half alone leaves an alert nobody can action or a queue nobody can work.

## Scope

- An SOS queue in the admin console: live list, acknowledge, resolve, with the trip and both parties reachable from the row.
- A payout queue reading E06's `payout_requests`: approve marks paid and debits, reject returns the request
  with a reason. **Call the debit primitive E06 exports from `wallet.ts` — do not re-implement it here and do
  not edit `wallet.ts`.** Root R1 is fifteen findings of exactly that mistake.
- Reason capture on both — a destructive action that cannot record why is how F-11-08 happened.
- Wire `routes/admin.ts` endpoints for both. Do not build the trip-detail timeline here; that is Wave 2.

## Acceptance criteria

- An operator can see an SOS within seconds of it firing and mark it handled.
- An operator can pay or reject a payout, and the captain's balance moves only then.
- Neither action is possible without a recorded reason and actor.
