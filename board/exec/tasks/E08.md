> **Goal.** Pay the number that was agreed, and make money move through one function.

## Why this blocks launch

Two things, same file. First: on the direct-accept path the captain is shown `offered_price` and settlement resolves `estimated_fare` (T05 F-05-01) — the author calls this the most important finding in that document because it breaks the product's differentiator quietly. Second: the codebase has a correct idiom for moving money (insert with `idempotency_key`, check `meta.changes === 1`, then move the balance) and uses it in two branches while three other call sites hand-roll it wrong — root R1 in the plan, 15 findings across 5 tracks. Under the cash-only launch shape the cash-commission debit at `trips.ts:1029-1033` is the only live money path, and it is the one debit in the codebase with no balance floor (F-03-11/F-04-12, promoted to G1).

## Scope

- Make `/trips/:id/accept` settle the price the captain was shown.
- Introduce `apps/api/src/lib/money.ts` with one `moveMoney()` primitive implementing the insert-as-lock idiom correctly, once.
- Route the cash-commission debit and the captain credit through it. Add the missing balance floor.
- Call `logAudit` (from E12's `lib/audit.ts`) on the completion path — `trips.ts` has imported it and never called it (T22 F-22-05).
- Leave the wallet-path mint in place but **quarantined behind E04's rejection** and commented as G1‡.

## Acceptance criteria

- A captain accepting an offer of X is credited from X, proven by a test.
- Every balance-moving statement in the file goes through `moveMoney()`.
- A cash commission larger than the captain's balance cannot drive it negative.
- A completed trip writes an audit row.
