> **Goal.** Let the rider client recover instead of stranding the user.

## Why this blocks launch

The server half is E09's; this is the client half and they are useless apart. `bootstrap()` never asks for an active trip, and `fare_estimate_sheet.dart` discards the `tripId` that the 409 carries — so the app has the information needed to recover and throws it away (T09 F-09-01). Separately the live trip shows the system estimate rather than the negotiated `accepted_price`, which is never read anywhere in the rider app (F-09-02) — in a price-negotiation product that reads as a bait-and-switch.

## Scope

- On bootstrap, call `GET /trips/active` and route straight to the live trip if one exists.
- Stop discarding the `tripId` in the 409 path; offer the rider the trip instead of an error.
- Render `accepted_price` when it exists, falling back to the estimate only when it does not.

## Acceptance criteria

- Force-quitting mid-trip and reopening lands on the live trip.
- A rider with a stuck trip is offered recovery, never a dead end.
- The fare on screen during the ride is the fare that was agreed.
