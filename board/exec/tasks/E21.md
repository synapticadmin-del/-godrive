> **Goal.** The number on screen during the ride is the number the rider agreed to pay.

## Provenance

Created **after** the original twenty, at the board owner's instruction, when **E10 reported seam
#13** on PR #107. The same remedy the board already used once: **E20** was added post-round-1 for
the orphaned second half of gate item 8 (`WAVE-PLAN.md` §10). A gate item whose effect lands in a
file no live task owns needs an owner, not a workaround.

## Why this blocks launch

`trip_screen.dart` rendered `estimated_fare` in the `assigned`/`arrived` panel (`:623`) and the
`in_progress` panel (`:651`). **Both are reached only after a captain has accepted**, which is the
moment `accepted_price` is written — by the bid path and by E08's settlement primitive on direct
accept. So the agreed price always existed and was never read: `accepted_price` appeared **nowhere**
in `apps/rider/`. The rider negotiates one number and watches a different one for the whole ride
(T09 F-09-02). In a price-negotiation product that is a trust defect, not a display bug.

`:671` (`completed`) read `final_fare ?? estimated_fare` — not wrong in practice, since E08 writes
both, but a third divergent copy of the precedence.

## Scope

- One helper defining the fare precedence **once**; the three panels call it.
- Live trip: `accepted_price` wins, `estimated_fare` only as the pre-agreement fallback.
- Completion: `final_fare` still wins — settlement may adjust the agreed price for waiting or tolls.

## Acceptance criteria

- On a trip whose accepted price differs from the estimate, the `assigned`, `arrived` and
  `in_progress` panels all show the **accepted** price.
- The completed panel still shows `final_fare`.
- `searching`/`offered` — where nothing has been agreed — still show the estimate.
