> **Goal.** Make the launch shape real at the API, not just in the client.

## Why this blocks launch

The rider app hardcodes `'cash'`, but `trips.ts:468` reads `body.paymentMethod || "cash"` and `payments.ts:12-18` accepts a caller-chosen `amount` and `tripId` with no ownership or fare check — both verified on main. Anyone who can make an HTTP request can therefore reach the wallet and card paths that §2.1 of the plan descopes, including the money mint (F-18-01) and the fare bypass (F-04-03). A client-side launch shape is not a launch shape.

## Scope

- Narrow the payment-method enum in `apps/api/src/lib/schemas.ts` to `cash` only, with a comment naming the plan section and the findings this neutralises.
- Narrow `intentionSchema` in `apps/api/src/routes/payments.ts` the same way; the endpoint should reject rather than 500.
- Make `routes/intercity.ts` and `routes/companies.ts` return a clear 404/501 even if remounted by accident.
- Leave the underlying defects unfixed and **listed** — they are G1‡ in the plan's ledger, meaning disabled, not repaired.

## Acceptance criteria

- `POST /trips` with `paymentMethod: "wallet"` or `"card"` is rejected with a 4xx and creates nothing.
- `POST /payments/paymob/intention` is rejected for every purpose.
- A test asserts each rejection, so re-enabling is a deliberate act that turns a test red.
