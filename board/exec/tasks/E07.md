> **Goal.** Stop billing corporate customers wrong money every minute.

## Why this blocks launch

The monthly B2B invoice job runs on the every-minute cron, and its `SUM` and its settling `UPDATE` disagree about timestamp format, so first-day trips are re-invoiced every minute (T08 F-08-01). A second generator in `companies.ts:167` has the mirror defect and under-bills (F-08-20). T20 reproduced all three billing outcomes in SQLite (F-20-16). This is automatic, unalerted, and pointed at paying customers.

## Scope

- Disable the invoice job in `apps/api/src/cron/invoices.ts` (the file E02 created). Disabled, with a comment naming the findings and the re-enable condition.
- Collapse the generator **in your file** to one `generateCompanyInvoice()` helper for when it returns — but
  do not re-enable it in this task. **The mirror generator in `companies.ts:167` is E04's file, not yours.**
  E04 disables the companies vertical outright, which kills that path; do not edit it.
- Call E12's `pingDeadMan()` from this job's success path. E12 exports the helper and must not edit your file.
- Note in the PR how many invoice rows already exist, so someone can assess the damage.

## Acceptance criteria

- The every-minute cron performs no billing.
- There is exactly one *live* invoice-generation code path — yours, disabled. The `companies.ts` copy is
  dead by E04's vertical shutdown, not by your edit. Gate item 5 closes only when both halves are verified.
- Re-enabling requires deleting an explicit guard, not flipping a subtle condition.
