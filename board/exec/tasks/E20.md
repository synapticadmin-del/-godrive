## Why this exists

This task was created on 2026-08-01, after `E11` finished and reported that gate item 8 could not be
closed on PR #91. `WAVE-PLAN` §4 listed item 8 as single-task; it is not. E11 owns the captain end and
`packages/flutter_shared`; the two screens that actually **render** the car belong to neither, and were
in nobody's `owns:` list.

The consequence is the one ordering trap §6 of `WAVE-PLAN` warns about, arriving from the other
direction: **E11 lengthens the publish interval to 20/min.** That is strictly better data — it stops the
captain being throttled and dropped from dispatch — but with an unchanged marker it makes the rider's
experience *worse*, because the gap between fixes gets longer and the car jumps further each time. E11
shipped `AnimatedVehicleMarker` precisely so this could not happen, and then could not call it.

## Scope

- Replace both `VehicleMapMarker` call sites with E11's `AnimatedVehicleMarker`, driving it from
  `LatLngTween` / `go_motion.dart` so position **and** bearing interpolate between fixes.
- **Interpolate, never extrapolate.** E11's widget is built on this rule and T13/T28 state it
  independently: a predicted position that turns out wrong is a car that visibly reverses. Animate
  towards the last known fix over roughly the publish interval; do not project past it.
- Take `heading` from the payload if the socket carries it, and derive bearing from consecutive fixes
  when it does not. Do not rotate on a zero-length delta — a stationary car must not spin.
- On the home map, key the markers by captain id so a poll updates positions instead of rebuilding the
  list. An unkeyed wholesale replace cannot animate by construction.
- Respect reduce-motion. `flutter_shared` already exposes the contract; T13 found the rider app is the
  one that *has* reduce-motion and the captain app that does not, so there is no excuse here.

## Acceptance criteria

- With fixes arriving every ~3 s, the car moves continuously between them on both screens. Recording or
  frame timings on the PR; "added the widget" is not the acceptance criterion, the motion is.
- The car points the way it is travelling, and does not rotate while stationary.
- Reduce-motion disables the tween and falls back to a direct position update, without exceptions thrown.
- `flutter analyze` clean for `apps/rider` — that is what `ci.yml` runs.
