> **Goal.** Three client changes that stop the product lying to people.

## Why this blocks launch

The `سفر` (intercity) chip books an ordinary city trip, so a rider asking for a 220 km journey silently enters city dispatch (T20 F-20-01, live today). The rider SOS screen hardcodes `AppTokens.lightPanel` and renders a full-brightness white panel at night — the captain app already uses the correct `sosBackdrop` token (T12 F-12-03). And `sos_screen.dart:33` tells the rider the authorities will be notified, while nothing in either app can dial 122/123/180 (T17 F-17-06).

## Scope

- Disable the `سفر` chip in `vehicle_selector.dart` — visibly unavailable, not silently rerouted.
- Swap the rider SOS screen to the dark-aware token the captain app already uses.
- Delete the sentence claiming authorities are notified. Replace it with what actually happens today: the platform's operators are alerted. Do not overstate that either.

## Acceptance criteria

- Selecting intercity is impossible from the rider app.
- The rider SOS screen is legible in dark mode.
- No copy in either app claims emergency services are contacted.
