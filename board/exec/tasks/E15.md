> **Goal.** Stop routing the business through a server that may switch it off.

## Why this blocks launch

All three environments point `OSRM_URL` at `router.project-osrm.org` — verified on main at `wrangler.toml:88`, `:148`, `:176` — whose policy forbids this use and permits withdrawal without notice. When it fails, a bare `catch` in `routing.ts` silently substitutes haversine×1.35, and because a negotiated fare is never recomputed, that estimate **becomes the settled price** (F-21-02). It is simultaneously a legal transfer of precise trip coordinates to an uncontracted third party (F-25-08), an availability SPOF, and a silent mispricing engine. §5.1 of the plan rules on the provider question: self-host OSRM, and reject Google Places because its terms forbid rendering its results on the non-Google map both apps use.

## Scope

- Stand up OSRM from the Geofabrik Egypt extract (~169 MB, MLD pipeline) behind a health-checked hostname; point all three environments at it.
- Make `OSRM_URL` fail closed — no working public default. Booting production without it is an error, not a fallback.
- Add an `allowFallback` parameter to `getRoute` **defaulting to today's behaviour**, plus the `source` field
  and a `ROUTE_UNAVAILABLE` failure mode. **Do not edit the call sites.** They are `trips.ts:331` (estimate)
  and `trips.ts:382` (booking), which E09 owns. Your PR must be safe to merge with them untouched.
- Flipping booking to `allowFallback: false` and returning 503 is **E09's line** — gate item 11 is not closed
  until both halves are verified. Say so in your PR so the verifier does not close it early.
- Persist `route_source` on the trip row: **you ship the migration** (take the number from the lock) and the
  value on the `getRoute` return. **The write at the trip INSERT site is E09's** — it is in `trips.ts`, the file
  you are already forbidden from touching. Same split as `allowFallback`.
- Add the two-line `viewbox` bias to search while you are here — T21 measured Nominatim returning results up to ~700 km away.

## Acceptance criteria

- No environment references `router.project-osrm.org`.
- `getRoute` accepts `allowFallback` and behaves exactly as before when it is not passed.
- A forced routing outage produces an alert and a failed booking, never a straight-line price.
- Every trip row records which engine priced it.
