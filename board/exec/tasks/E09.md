> **Goal.** Stop the product from bricking rider accounts.

## Why this blocks launch

A trip nobody accepts never leaves `searching` — no timeout, no terminal state, no sweeper — and the `ACTIVE_TRIP` guard then refuses to let that rider book again (T06 F-06-04, T09 F-09-01, T16 F-16-01, T27 F-27-06: four tracks, one hole). Requesting a ride when no captain is online is the *normal* early outcome for a platform bootstrapping supply. Scheduled rides make it routine: they dispatch at booking time rather than scheduled time, exhaust the wave rollout hours early, and land in exactly that state (F-06-01/F-16-02).

## Scope

- Add a trip-expiry sweeper to `apps/api/src/cron/dispatch.ts`: `searching`/`offered` past a TTL move to a terminal unfulfilled state and the rider is told.
- Add `GET /trips/active` so a client can recover; return the trip id in the 409 body too.
- Gate live dispatch on `!scheduledFor`, and have the cron dispatch scheduled trips at their scheduled time — or, if that is not finishable in this task, disable scheduling at the schema and say so.
- Use `lib/dispatch.ts` (E03's extraction) for both paths rather than duplicating the sequence.

## Acceptance criteria

- A trip with no captain reaches a terminal state without human intervention, and the rider can book again.
- Killing the app mid-trip and reopening returns the rider to the live trip.
- A trip scheduled for +9h dispatches at +9h, or cannot be created at all.
