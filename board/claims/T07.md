## Progress
- Reviewed the whole realtime surface at base commit `f906e0b06240ea84b20ef5c7b5633fd9abe02f8b`: all four Durable Objects
  (`TripRoom`, `CaptainInbox`, `GeoCell`, `OfferScheduler`), both `/ws/*` upgrade routes in `index.ts`, `wrangler.toml`,
  `rateLimit.ts`, the location/chat/dispatch producer paths in `trips.ts`/`captain.ts`/`safety.ts`, and all three Flutter
  socket clients plus the screens that own them. 28 findings (6 S1, 10 S2, 9 S3, 3 S4), every one citing a real `path:line`.
- **Headline S1:** the captain publishes location on a 10 m distance filter (`captain_state.dart:625`) into a fixed-window
  cap of 30/min (`captain.ts:192-197`), so at 30 km/h the quota is spent in the first 22 s and the rider's car freezes for
  ~38 s before teleporting — every minute, all trip. The client swallows the 429 (`captain_state.dart:682`), so nothing
  measures it, and it cannot reproduce at walking pace. Two sensible decisions made independently that cannot both hold.
- **Second S1 cluster:** both socket-holding DOs use the Hibernation API (`ctx.acceptWebSocket`) while keeping every scrap
  of per-connection state in an in-memory `Map` with no `serializeAttachment`. After eviction the auth gate stops gating
  (`TripRoom.ts:153`), `broadcast()`'s second loop never checks `pendingAuth` (`:280-288`), and the `CaptainInbox` relay
  dies while still answering pings — a captain whose app looks perfectly healthy and receives no offers, ever.
- Also S1: every captain socket in the fleet enters through one shared DO `idFromName("pending-auth")` (`index.ts:226`) —
  a single-threaded, never-hibernating, fleet-wide SPOF; the rider can never receive a chat message live (zero
  `chat.message` handlers in `apps/rider/lib`) while `safety.ts:173-177` asserts "Both apps subscribe"; and no role check
  on inbound `location` frames lets a rider forge the captain's position.
- Two brief premises corrected by evidence: `OfferScheduler` alarms are **bounded**, not an eternal billing leak (it is the
  best-built object in the review), and PR #47's TripRoom-id fix is **half** complete — the Worker forwards `?tripId=` on
  the pending-auth branch only. Cost model quantified: ~$5.63/hour of DO duration at 1,000 concurrent trips versus under
  $0.03/hour once hibernation is allowed to engage, and a rider backstop poll costing 7× the socket it protects.
