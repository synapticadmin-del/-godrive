## Questions you must answer

1. Notification infrastructure: the queue is configured with batching, retries and a DLQ. Who consumes it, what is in the DLQ today, and is anyone alerted when messages land there? Is delivery observable at all (sent, delivered, opened)?
2. Channel strategy: FCM push, WhatsApp, SMS, email. Which are live versus stubbed? WhatsApp is the dominant channel in Egypt — is it wired for anything beyond OTP?
3. Notification taxonomy: list every notification the system can send today, and design the complete set with, for each: trigger, channel, timing, copy (Arabic), deep link, and whether it is transactional or promotional.
4. Preferences and compliance: can a user opt out of marketing while keeping transactional? Is there a quiet-hours policy? Sending a promo at 2 a.m. loses the install.
5. Deep links: does tapping a notification land in the right screen with the right state? Is there any deep-link handling in the Flutter apps at all? Specify the URL scheme and the routes.
6. Referrals: the `referrals` and `user_credits` tables exist and there is an `invite_screen.dart`. Is the loop complete — code generation, attribution, reward on qualifying event, fraud guard (coordinate with T18)? Design the full mechanic with concrete reward values and payback maths.
7. Promo mechanics: what types are supported (percentage, fixed, first-ride, ride-count, corridor-specific, time-boxed)? What is missing for a real growth calendar?
8. Captain supply growth: referral for captains, onboarding incentives, quest/streak bonuses, and guaranteed-earnings promotions. None appear to exist. Design the programme and its cost model.
9. Activation funnel: define the events to instrument from install to first completed trip, for both apps, and the drop-off points to watch. (Coordinate with T22 for the telemetry pipeline.)
10. Retention: define the lifecycle campaigns — day-1 unactivated, day-7 lapsed, post-first-trip, weekly habit, win-back at 30 days — with the trigger conditions and the message for each.
11. Ratings prompts and store presence: when to ask for a store review, and how the app-store listing supports acquisition (coordinate with T26).
12. Attribution: is there any install-attribution capability? What is the minimum needed to know which channel produced a rider?

- inDrive: growth driven by price transparency and referral loops rather than paid acquisition.

## Shared context (identical for every task)

**Synaptic Go** is an Egyptian ride-hailing platform, currently pre-production.
The product goal is a transport and delivery app with the feature depth of Uber
and the price-negotiation model of inDrive, hardened to full production quality.

**Stack.** Cloudflare Workers (Hono-style routing) + D1 (SQLite) + KV
(`SESSIONS`) + R2 (`FILES`) + four Durable Objects (`TripRoom`, `GeoCell`,
`CaptainInbox`, `OfferScheduler`) + Queues (`NOTIFICATIONS` with a DLQ) + two
cron triggers (scheduled-trip dispatch every minute, B2B invoices monthly).
Two Flutter apps (`apps/rider`, `apps/captain`) sharing `packages/flutter_shared`.
A React + Vite admin console (`apps/admin`). 19 D1 migrations. `APP_VERSION 0.4.0`.

**API surface.** `apps/api/src/routes/`: admin, auth, captain, companies,
devices, geocode, intercity, payments, promo, safety, search, trips, user,
wallet. `apps/api/src/lib/`: audit, cleanup, geocode, jwt, nearby, notifications,
paymob, pricing, routing, schemas, turnstile, types, utils.
