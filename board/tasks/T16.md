## Questions you must answer

1. Build the feature register. For each capability, mark: implemented / partial / absent, with evidence. Cover at minimum — multi-stop trips (waypoints column exists: is it wired end to end?), scheduled rides, recurring rides, ride for someone else, favourite drivers, driver preferences, women-only or female-driver matching, pet/luggage options, child seat, accessibility vehicles, ride sharing/pooling, corporate ride tagging, tipping, split fare, in-trip destination change, in-trip stop addition, waiting-time charging, cancellation windows and fees, no-show flow, lost and found, receipts and invoices, trip rating and feedback, driver compliments, re-book from history, saved places (home/work), pickup notes, gate/building instructions, airport and venue pickup zones, toll handling, night pricing, and estimated-vs-final fare reconciliation.
2. Trip state machine: enumerate every status value in the code, the legal transitions, who can trigger each, and the timeouts. Identify states that can be entered but never left, and transitions with no server-side guard.
3. Cancellation: who can cancel, when, what it costs, and what happens to the money. PR #7 mentions a cancellation delay. Map the current rules and design the complete policy including grace period, captain-arrived state, and repeat-offender handling.
4. Scheduled rides: `scheduled_for` plus a one-minute cron exist. How far in advance can a rider book, when does dispatch start, what if no captain accepts, and is the rider notified? Is the fare locked at booking or at dispatch?
5. Multi-stop: waypoints are stored as JSON — is there UI, routing, pricing, and captain-side sequencing? If partial, specify the missing pieces.
6. Search and places: `routes/search.ts` and saved places — assess POI coverage for Cairo, Arabic search quality, recent destinations, and the Nominatim dependency's suitability (rate limits, licence, Arabic results).
7. Trip history and receipts: what does a rider get after a trip? Is there a downloadable/emailable receipt? A tax invoice for corporate riders?
8. Ratings: is rating two-way, mandatory, and does a low rating do anything? Where is the aggregate stored and displayed?
9. Delivery and courier vertical: the product goal mentions delivery. Nothing in the schema suggests parcels. Scope what a courier vertical would require on top of the current trip model (coordinate with T20).
10. For every absent feature, estimate effort and place it in a sequence that reaches a credible launch, then parity, then differentiation.

## Shared context (identical for every task)

**Synaptic Go** is an Egyptian ride-hailing platform, currently pre-production.
The product goal is a transport and delivery app with the feature depth of Uber
and the price-negotiation model of inDrive, hardened to full production quality.

**Stack.** Cloudflare Workers (Hono-style routing) + D1 (SQLite) + KV
(`SESSIONS`) + R2 (`FILES`) + four Durable Objects (`TripRoom`, `GeoCell`,
`CaptainInbox`, `OfferScheduler`) + Queues (`NOTIFICATIONS` with a DLQ) + two
cron triggers (scheduled-trip dispatch every minute, B2B invoices monthly).
Two Flutter apps (`apps/rider`, `apps/captain`) sharing `packages/flutter_shared`.
A React + Vite admin console (`apps/admin`). 19 D1 migrations. `APP_VERSION 0.4.0`.

**API surface.** `apps/api/src/routes/`: admin, auth, captain, companies,
devices, geocode, intercity, payments, promo, safety, search, trips, user,
wallet. `apps/api/src/lib/`: audit, cleanup, geocode, jwt, nearby, notifications,
paymob, pricing, routing, schemas, turnstile, types, utils.
`apps/api/src/middleware/`: auth, rateLimit.

**Existing planning docs** — read the ones relevant to your track and write *on
top of* them rather than restating them: `docs/ROADMAP.md`, `docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md`,
`docs/CHECKLIST.md`, `docs/BIDDING_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/API.md`,
`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/STACK.md`, `docs/COST.md`, `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`.
