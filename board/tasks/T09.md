## Questions you must answer

1. Map the complete navigation graph and the trip state machine as the app models it. Where does the app's model diverge from the server's? PR #54 unified a duplicated waiting screen — are there other duplicated or unreachable states?
2. First run: permissions (location, notifications), what the app shows before permission is granted, and whether a denial is recoverable without reinstalling.
3. The core loop — set pickup, set destination, see estimate, offer a price, receive bids, choose a captain, watch them arrive, ride, pay, rate. Time and count every tap. Where is it longer than inDrive's?
4. State management: `app_state.dart` — is it one god-object? Rebuild scope, listener leaks (PR #36 fixed two controller leaks — look for more), and what happens to in-flight state when the app is killed and reopened mid-trip.
5. Error, empty, loading and offline states for every screen. Which screens show a spinner forever on failure? Is there a single retry pattern or twelve?
6. Location accuracy: pin placement, address reverse-geocode race (PR #3 fixed one), GPS drift in dense Cairo streets, and the ability to correct a wrong pickup point.
7. Offline and poor-network behaviour: 2G/3G, tunnel, lift. What is cached, what queues, what is lost? `offline_gate.dart` and `offline_guard_banner.dart` exist — where are they actually used?
8. Trip screen fidelity: does the car marker move smoothly or teleport? Is ETA live? Is the driver's real photo and plate visible (PR #51 added photo and routed ETA — verify coverage)?
9. Post-trip: receipt detail, dispute path, lost-item path, tipping, re-book, favourite driver. Which exist?
10. Notifications: what does the rider receive, when, and can they act from the notification? Deep links into a live trip?
11. Performance: cold start time, jank on the map screen, memory on a 2 GB Android device, APK size.
12. Localisation in practice: Arabic-first layout, RTL correctness in every custom widget, and text overflow with long Arabic strings.

## Benchmark against

- inDrive rider: price-first flow, offer entry as the primary action, bid list with driver ratings and ETA.
- Uber rider: upfront price, one-tap rebooking, saved places surfaced predictively, live activity on the lock screen.

## Shared context (identical for every task)

**Synaptic Go** is an Egyptian ride-hailing platform, currently pre-production.
The product goal is a transport and delivery app with the feature depth of Uber
and the price-negotiation model of inDrive, hardened to full production quality.

**Stack.** Cloudflare Workers (Hono-style routing) + D1 (SQLite) + KV
(`SESSIONS`) + R2 (`FILES`) + four Durable Objects (`TripRoom`, `GeoCell`,
`CaptainInbox`, `OfferScheduler`) + Queues (`NOTIFICATIONS` with a DLQ) + two
cron triggers (scheduled-trip dispatch every minute, B2B invoices monthly).
Two Flutter apps (`apps/rider`, `apps/captain`) sharing `packages/flutter_shared`.
A React + Vite admin console (`apps/admin`). 19 D1 migrations. `APP_VERSION 0.4.0`.

**API surface.** `apps/api/src/routes/`: admin, auth, captain, companies,
devices, geocode, intercity, payments, promo, safety, search, trips, user,
wallet. `apps/api/src/lib/`: audit, cleanup, geocode, jwt, nearby, notifications,
paymob, pricing, routing, schemas, turnstile, types, utils.
`apps/api/src/middleware/`: auth, rateLimit.
