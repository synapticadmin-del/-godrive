## Mission

Prove, or disprove, that money in this system is conserved. Every piastre that enters the wallet, moves between rider / captain / platform, or leaves as a payout must be traceable, double-entry-consistent, idempotent under retry, and impossible to conjure through a race.

## Questions you must answer

1. Is the ledger genuinely double-entry, or a running balance column with a transaction log beside it? If balance is denormalised, what reconciles it, and what happens when they diverge?
2. Currency representation: migration 0005 moved to integer currency — is that complete? Search for any remaining float arithmetic on money, and every rounding site. Where does the half-piastre go?
3. Idempotency: which write paths accept an idempotency key, how long are keys retained, and what happens on the same key with a *different* body? Is the key scoped per user?
4. Concurrency: two simultaneous debits against a balance of 50 EGP for 40 EGP each — does D1 prevent the double spend? Is there a transaction, a conditional UPDATE with a WHERE guard, or nothing? Quote the actual SQL.
5. Trip settlement: at completion, what exactly happens — rider debit, captain credit, platform commission — and is it one atomic unit? What is the state if the worker dies between steps? Is there a reconciliation job?
6. Commission: where is the rate defined, can it change mid-trip, is it stored on the trip row at accept-time or read live at completion?
7. Negative balances: can a captain's wallet go negative (cash trip owing commission)? Is that intended, bounded, and collectible?
8. Cash trips: how is a cash fare represented in the ledger, and how does the platform collect its cut? This is the dominant payment method in Egypt — it must be modelled, not assumed away.
9. Refunds and cancellations: is there any refund path at all? Trace `migrations/0010_intercity_booking_cancel.sql` — seat refunds exist for intercity; does the equivalent exist for city trips?
10. Promo and credit interaction: can a discount plus wallet credit exceed the fare and mint money? Check ordering of promo application vs commission calculation.
11. Auditability: given a captain disputing yesterday's earnings, can ops reconstruct the day from the ledger alone? What is missing to make that possible?

## Benchmark against

- Uber: immutable double-entry ledger, every trip produces balanced journal entries, daily automated reconciliation against PSP settlement files.
- inDrive: cash-dominant markets with captain commission debt tracking and automatic go-offline when debt exceeds a threshold.
- Egyptian reality: cash is king, wallet top-up via Paymob/Fawry/Vodafone Cash, captains distrust opaque deductions — the earnings breakdown must be legible.
