## Questions you must answer

1. Time-to-first-trip: count every step from install to receiving a first offer. PR #38 fixed nine defects in the registration wizard — walk it again and find what remains. Where does a real Egyptian driver drop out?
2. Document onboarding: the dynamic catalogue (0014, 0015), upload UX on a phone camera, file size and format handling, rejection feedback (PR with `RejectionReasonModal` on admin side), and re-upload. How long does a captain wait in limbo, and does the app tell them?
3. The offer moment: an offer arrives while driving. How many seconds to decide, how large is the tap target, is the sound and haptic adequate, and can they accept without looking? Assess `offer_card.dart` against that bar.
4. Counter-offer UX: how many taps to counter, are there preset increments, and is the expected earning after commission shown *before* they commit?
5. Active trip: navigation handoff to Google Maps/Waze (`navigation_button.dart`), arrival confirmation, start/end trip, waiting timer, and multi-stop handling. What is missing to complete a trip without touching another app?
6. Earnings transparency: does the captain see gross, commission, net, and the reason for every deduction? Daily and weekly totals? Payout status? Distrust here is the number-one driver-churn cause in this market.
7. Online/offline control: is the state honest? PR #4 added a guard and PR #49 fixed search radius. What does the captain see about *why* they are not getting trips (no demand vs offline vs documents pending vs debt)?
8. Battery and data: location update frequency, WebSocket keepalive, wake locks, foreground service on Android, and behaviour when the OS kills the app. PR #23 added battery saving — measure what it actually does. Estimate mAh/hour and MB/hour.
9. Low-end device reality: test-reason about 2 GB RAM, Android 9, cracked screen, gloves, direct sunlight. Contrast, font size, and tap targets under those conditions.
10. Safety for the driver: SOS reachability while driving, incident reporting, and passenger-rating visibility before accepting.
11. Offline resilience: trip in progress, network dies for five minutes. Does the app hold state and resync, or lose the trip?
12. Session longevity: is the captain logged out unexpectedly? Ten-hour shifts must not require re-auth.

## Shared context (identical for every task)

**Synaptic Go** is an Egyptian ride-hailing platform, currently pre-production.
The product goal is a transport and delivery app with the feature depth of Uber
and the price-negotiation model of inDrive, hardened to full production quality.

**Stack.** Cloudflare Workers (Hono-style routing) + D1 (SQLite) + KV
(`SESSIONS`) + R2 (`FILES`) + four Durable Objects (`TripRoom`, `GeoCell`,
`CaptainInbox`, `OfferScheduler`) + Queues (`NOTIFICATIONS` with a DLQ) + two
cron triggers (scheduled-trip dispatch every minute, B2B invoices monthly).
Two Flutter apps (`apps/rider`, `apps/captain`) sharing `packages/flutter_shared`.
A React + Vite admin console (`apps/admin`). 19 D1 migrations. `APP_VERSION 0.4.0`.

**API surface.** `apps/api/src/routes/`: admin, auth, captain, companies,
devices, geocode, intercity, payments, promo, safety, search, trips, user,
wallet. `apps/api/src/lib/`: audit, cleanup, geocode, jwt, nearby, notifications,
paymob, pricing, routing, schemas, turnstile, types, utils.
`apps/api/src/middleware/`: auth, rateLimit.
