## Mission

This platform holds national IDs, driving licences, live location traces and payment records for Egyptian citizens. Audit how that data is handled against Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law and basic decency, and specify what must exist before launch.

## Questions you must answer

1. Data inventory: every category of personal data the platform holds — identity documents, phone numbers, precise location history, trip history, chat messages, payment tokens, device identifiers. For each: where it is stored, who can access it, how long it is kept, and whether it is encrypted at rest and in transit.
2. Egyptian PDPL (Law 151 of 2020): summarise the obligations that actually apply — lawful basis, consent, data-subject rights, breach notification timelines, cross-border transfer restrictions, and the data-protection officer requirement. Then map each obligation to what the product must implement. Be concrete about what is missing.
3. Cross-border transfer: user coordinates go to `router.project-osrm.org` and Nominatim; notifications go to Meta and Resend; data sits in Cloudflare's global network. Which of these are transfers under PDPL, and what is required to make them lawful?
4. Document storage: captain IDs and licences in R2 — access control, URL signing, retention after a captain leaves, and whether deleted means deleted. Can a support agent download every driver's national ID today?
5. Location history: `trip_path_points` is a movement trace of identifiable people. What is the retention justification, who can query it, and is it anonymised or deleted on schedule? This is the most sensitive dataset in the system.
6. PII in logs: does any log line, error message, audit row, or third-party payload contain a phone number, name, or coordinate? Search for it.
7. Consent and transparency: is there a privacy policy, terms of service, or consent capture anywhere in the apps? Is consent recorded with a timestamp and version? Currently this appears entirely absent — specify what is needed and where it goes in the onboarding flow.
8. Data-subject rights: can a user request access, correction, deletion, or export? Design the mechanism, including what must be retained despite a deletion request (financial records for tax, trip records for disputes) and the legal basis for keeping it.
9. Staff access: is there any policy or logging around who views a user's data in the admin? Access to PII should itself be audited (coordinate with T02 and T11).
10. Retention schedule: produce a table — every data category, retention period, legal basis, and the deletion mechanism. Then check whether `cleanup.ts` implements any of it.
11. Breach response: what would the team do if the D1 database leaked? Notification obligations, timelines, and the runbook.
12. Other regimes: driver classification and labour law implications, VAT and e-invoicing requirements for the Egyptian Tax Authority, transport-sector licensing, and insurance obligations. Flag what needs legal counsel rather than engineering — but flag it clearly and specifically.

- Egypt PDPL 151/2020 and its executive regulations — cite the articles that apply.
- GDPR as the design template, since it is stricter and future-proofs expansion.
- Uber and Careem's published privacy centres as the transparency standard to aim for.

## Shared context (identical for every task)

**Synaptic Go** is an Egyptian ride-hailing platform, currently pre-production.
The product goal is a transport and delivery app with the feature depth of Uber
and the price-negotiation model of inDrive, hardened to full production quality.

**Stack.** Cloudflare Workers (Hono-style routing) + D1 (SQLite) + KV
(`SESSIONS`) + R2 (`FILES`) + four Durable Objects (`TripRoom`, `GeoCell`,
`CaptainInbox`, `OfferScheduler`) + Queues (`NOTIFICATIONS` with a DLQ) + two
cron triggers (scheduled-trip dispatch every minute, B2B invoices monthly).
Two Flutter apps (`apps/rider`, `apps/captain`) sharing `packages/flutter_shared`.
A React + Vite admin console (`apps/admin`). 19 D1 migrations. `APP_VERSION 0.4.0`.

**API surface.** `apps/api/src/routes/`: admin, auth, captain, companies,
devices, geocode, intercity, payments, promo, safety, search, trips, user,
wallet. `apps/api/src/lib/`: audit, cleanup, geocode, jwt, nearby, notifications,
paymob, pricing, routing, schemas, turnstile, types, utils.
