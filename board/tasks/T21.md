## Questions you must answer

1. Dependency risk: `OSRM_URL` points at `router.project-osrm.org` — the public demo server. Read its usage policy. It is not licensed for production load, has no SLA, and will rate-limit or block. Same question for Nominatim. Quantify the risk and cost the alternatives (self-hosted OSRM on a VM, Mapbox, Google, MapTiler, Valhalla) with concrete pricing at 1,000 and 10,000 trips/day.
2. Fallback quality: when OSRM fails the code falls back to haversine. What is the straight-line-to-road-distance error in Cairo (the Nile crossings alone make it severe)? Quantify it and state the fare impact — this is a money bug, not a UX one.
3. ETA accuracy: is traffic considered at all? OSRM's default profile has no live traffic. What is the p50/p90 ETA error in Cairo rush hour, and what would fix it?
4. Caching: geocode results cached in KV for 30d/7d. Is routing cached? What is the cache key granularity, and what is the hit rate likely to be? Design a coordinate-rounding scheme that raises it.
5. Geocoding quality in Arabic: does Nominatim return usable Arabic results for Egyptian addresses, informal area names ('عزبة', 'كوبري', landmark-relative addressing)? Egyptians navigate by landmarks, not street numbers — does the search support that? This may be the single biggest usability gap in the product.
6. Map tiles: what provider serves tiles now, under what licence, and what is the cost at scale? Is attribution correct?
7. Route geometry: `route_geometry` is stored per trip — encoding format, size, and whether it is used for replay, dispute resolution, and fare verification.
8. Path points: sampled every 30 s. Is that enough to reconstruct a route for a dispute? Is snap-to-road applied? What is the storage cost (coordinate with T08)?
9. Pickup precision: how is the pickup point chosen and corrected? Are there curated pickup zones for airports, malls, and stadiums where GPS is unreliable?
10. Client-side location: accuracy settings, permission handling, background behaviour, and battery cost in `location_service.dart` and `captain_state.dart` (coordinate with T10).
11. Offline maps: any caching for tunnels and dead zones?
12. Geofencing: city boundaries (`captain_city`, `DEFAULT_CITY=cairo`) — how are they defined, and what happens at the edge or on an intercity trip?
