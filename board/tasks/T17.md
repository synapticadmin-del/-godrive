## Questions you must answer

1. SOS: trace what actually happens when the button is pressed — who is notified, how fast, with what payload, and is there any human on the other end? A button that emails nobody is worse than no button. Assess honestly and design the real flow including an ops runbook.
2. Trip sharing: `trip_share_tokens` allows unauthenticated tracking. What PII does the public view expose (captain's full name, plate, phone, rider's home address)? Token entropy, expiry, and revocation on trip end.
3. In-trip chat: is it retained, moderated, and available to support during a dispute? Can phone numbers be exchanged (and should they be)? Is there number masking for voice calls, or do the parties see each other's real numbers?
4. Identity assurance for captains: what documents are collected (0012, 0014), who verifies them, is there any liveness or selfie-match check, and is there periodic re-verification? Can a verified captain hand their account to someone else — and what would detect that?
5. Rider identity: is a rider anything more than a phone number? What stops a banned rider from returning with a new SIM? (Coordinate with T18.)
6. Ratings: is it two-way, and does anything happen at a low rating — automatic review, suspension threshold, or nothing? Where are aggregates computed and are they gameable?
7. Incident workflow: a rider reports harassment. What exists today — a form, a category taxonomy, an ops queue, an SLA, a suspension mechanism, a record for law enforcement? Design the whole path.
8. Blocking: can a rider or captain block each other and never be matched again? This is table stakes and appears absent.
9. Trip anomaly detection: long stop, route deviation, trip ending far from the destination, speeding. What could be detected from `trip_path_points` and what should trigger a check-in?
10. Emergency integration: Egyptian emergency numbers, and whether the app can share live location with a responder.
11. Vehicle safety: is the vehicle verified — registration, insurance, inspection date — and does an expiry block dispatch?
12. Women's safety: given the market, evaluate female-rider-specific protections (female captain preference, trusted contacts, discreet SOS) and make a recommendation.
